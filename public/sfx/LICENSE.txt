NightLight template sound effects

These files are cut and levelled from the recordings below by
scripts/sfx/process-sfx.mjs (trimmed, faded, layered, bit-crushed and
normalised; nothing is resampled musically). The original recordings are not
stored in this repository.

  impact.wav, punch.wav    Ghosthack "HTH FX Impact 09"
  zap.wav                  "Electric shock" + "Electricity charge sound effect"
  crackle.wav, glitch.wav  "biww short electric" (561891)
  whoosh.wav               "Fast woosh"
  swish.wav                "Whoosh Transition Whip-Swish Pan - Comical Sound Effects HD"
  swoosh.wav               "Swish Swoosh Cutscene Sound Effect"

the repository owner before this repository or the app is distributed. Sample
